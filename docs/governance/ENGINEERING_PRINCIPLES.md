# Engineering Principles

1. Truth before presentation.
2. Deterministic before probabilistic.
3. Provenance before convenience.
4. Local-first by default.
5. Read-only before write-enabled automation.
6. Small composable services over monoliths.
7. Every schema change is migratable.
8. Every release is reproducible.
9. Generated intelligence is visibly distinguished from canonical truth.
10. Humans remain final authority for engineering and safety decisions.
