# Reading Decision History

Decision history is the shortest path to understanding why NEOS or a project looks the way it does.

## Start with

- the decision title
- the decision status
- the decision rationale
- the related evidence
- any superseding decision

## Then ask

- what decision replaced this one?
- what changed because of this decision?
- what assumption supported it?
- what experiment validated it?
- what remains unknown?
